Be a part of the founding community who decides location, and how this festival is built.

Australasia Eclipse 2028 will be built with its community, not just sold to it. The first step is crowdfunding a location feasibility study: around 100 founding contributors at about $99 each, held in escrow by EdgeOS, to properly vet candidate sites from the Kimberley and the Red Centre to Sydney and New Zealand’s South Island for totality, access, safety, environmental impact and land permissions. Before we open it up, we want input from this trusted founding group on how it should work: how funding and voting relate, who makes the final call and by when, and what matters most in a site. You will see where everyone stands as soon as you vote.
